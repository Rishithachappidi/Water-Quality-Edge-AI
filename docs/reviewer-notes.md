# Revision / Reviewer Notes

This repository is a paper-based documentation repository.

Important revision points from the review process include:

- Acronyms should be expanded at first use.
- A related-work comparison table was added/planned at the end of Section 2,
  before the Proposed Methodology section.
- WQI/WQS terminology was standardized toward WQI.
- The manuscript discusses limitations including dataset size and lack of
  laboratory ground-truth validation.
- The manuscript was revised for figures, workflow presentation, literature
  positioning, contribution statements, conclusion, future work, and language.

Important unresolved or not fully reproducible items include:

- Full regression evaluation with RMSE, MAE, and R².
- Complete statistical significance testing.
- Full ablation studies.
- Broader multi-location/long-term experiments.
- Comprehensive laboratory validation.
- Complete quantitative edge-performance benchmarking.
- More extensive quantitative XAI evaluation.

These items should not be represented as completed experiments unless the
authors actually perform and document them.
