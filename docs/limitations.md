# Limitations

The manuscript/reviewer discussion identifies several limitations that should
remain transparent:

- The experimental dataset is relatively small.
- The study reports 120 pond readings and 6 tap-water readings.
- The available observations limit generalizability.
- Pollution observations are based on sensor readings rather than comprehensive
  laboratory ground-truth validation.
- Broader statistical validation would strengthen the findings.
- Longer-term and multi-location field validation is needed.
- More extensive quantitative XAI evaluation would strengthen the explainability
  claim.
- More detailed quantitative edge-performance benchmarking would strengthen the
  edge-AI evaluation.
